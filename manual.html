01 / WELCOME

Your home base.

The home page gives you one-click access to Pixel AI, Pixel Chat, Calculator and Chess Club. Use the sidebar to explore Mini Games, Big Games, TV, Music, My Games, Apps & browser, Notes and the Arcade helper.

All arcade screens live on this GitHub site: games, chat, accounts, stats, calculator, chess, AI and the developer TV gate. Shared accounts, messages and playtime use the Pixel service API behind the scenes. GitHub Pages itself only hosts static files. Embedded games, media, browsing and AI still depend on their providers.

02 / PRESS PLAY

Find a game. Make it yours.

  • Find: search for a name, choose a collection, use a category or try Surprise me.
  • Save favourites: click a game’s heart. Your list stays in this browser.
  • Launch: games normally open in a new about:blank tab and fill the window. Allow popups for Pixel Arcade. Change the launch mode to “Inside this page” in Settings if you prefer.
  • Game controls: open Controls in the game tab to restart, return to the library, use Quick switch or request browser fullscreen. True fullscreen needs your click because browsers require a user gesture.
  • Bring your own: My Games → Add a game accepts a URL, iframe embed, pasted HTML or an HTML file up to 50 MB. Only add content you trust. Some games need assets from their original host.
  • Progress: external games manage their own saves. Changing browser, origin or clearing site data can affect them. When signed in, embedded game playtime syncs to your arcade account.

San Andreas is a community free-roam engine, not the full story campaign; it requires working WebGL and a large first load. Falling Blocks and Dots & Boxes run directly from Pixel Arcade. Vice City uses its browser host’s setup and may download data into browser storage on its first run. Third-party games can change or become unavailable.

03 / MORE THAN GAMES

A few useful things.

Chess Club
Choose a bot and your colour. Click a piece, then a highlighted legal destination. Use hints, undo, promotion choices and PGN export. Bot rating labels are approximate.
Calculator
Choose a section from the grouped calculator menu: Standard, Scientific, History, Symbolic, Equations, Polynomials, Matrices, Vectors, Derivatives, Integrals, Diff. Eqs, QR Grapher, Statistics, Regression, Complex, Precision, Constants and Units. Enter the values and use the action button. History can be exported as text. Matrices and vectors use JSON arrays, such as [[1,2],[3,4]] and [1,2,3]. Equations use one variable, such as x^2-5*x+6=0. QR Grapher supports y=f(x) and implicit relations, such as x^2+y^2=25. Diff. Eqs solves first-order initial value problems numerically with RK4; increase the steps to check convergence. Definite integrals use a numerical estimate, while supported indefinite integrals are symbolic. Calculus uses radians. Precision offers 16–100 significant digits. Check the notes in each mode, domain restrictions and units before trusting a result.
Notes
Write in your scratchpad; notes save locally in this browser. Download a .txt copy to keep a backup.
Apps & browser
Enter a URL or search, add tabs with + and save bookmarks with ☆. Back/forward follow addresses you entered in the arcade; external page navigation belongs to Scramjet. Use the menu to open the browser in an arcade blank tab. Some websites reject embedding or require their own account.
Arcade helper
A simple catalogue guide for recommendations and site features. For open-ended questions, use Pixel AI.
04 / MEET YOUR ASSISTANT

A question is a good start.

  1. Open Pixel AI from Home, the sidebar or Apps.
  2. Wait for the service to load and press Connect with Puter. Complete sign-in in Puter’s own window. This is separate from your arcade account.
  3. Type a message and press Send or Enter. Shift + Enter adds a new line. Suggested prompts fill the message box; you choose when to send.
  4. Replies appear as they arrive. Ask a follow-up to continue. Recent successful messages provide context; long conversations eventually drop their oldest context.
  5. Use Stop to cancel a request, Retry last message after an error, or Copy on an answer. A partial or failed reply is not added to the next request’s context.
  6. New chat clears this tab’s conversation. Reloading also clears it. Disconnect signs out of the AI service for this app and clears this conversation.

Messages and recent context go to Puter and its AI provider. Usage draws from your Puter allowance or credits; check your Puter account for current limits and any costs. Pixel Arcade never asks you to paste an API key. Do not send passwords or private personal information.

The bot can explain, write, brainstorm and help with code. It cannot operate your games, read your private arcade account or verify live facts. Check important answers. New chat only clears the local view; provider records are governed by the provider’s policy.

05 / BRING YOUR FRIENDS

Your arcade. Your community.

Pixel Chat opens a full-window workspace here. Use the left server icons, channel sidebar and right member list to move around. Back to arcade returns home. The pin button opens pinned messages from the latest 100 loaded messages, the people button toggles the member list, and the smiley inserts emoji. Message actions appear when you hover a message or focus one of its buttons. Drafts are held in memory separately for each channel and clear when the page reloads. The colour icon opens Themes; the dim icon toggles reflection dimming.

Pixel Chat runs here in the arcade. Choose Account to sign in, then create a server, copy its invite code and share it with friends. Owners manage channels and members and pin messages. Text, reactions, editing and search are available; voice and video calls are not included.

Account opens the sign-in/profile panel here. My stats shows your saved account playtime here. Existing arcade accounts continue to work. New account passwords must contain at least 7 characters. GitHub sign-in is held in this tab’s session storage for up to one day; Log out revokes it. Your arcade account and Puter sign-in are separate.

Every signed-in account automatically joins Pixel Community, the shared public lobby. It opens by default, needs no invite and cannot be left. You can still create private servers and share their invite codes. No Discord membership or verification is required.

06 / MAKE IT COMFORTABLE

Set the mood.

Use Light / Dark in the header to switch themes. Settings also offers Use device setting. Your choice saves in this browser and syncs to other open arcade tabs. Open Themes in the header or Chat themes in the chat toolbar to choose Classic, Midnight, Aurora, Ocean, Matcha, Sunset, Rose Quartz or AMOLED. Each palette supports light, dark and device colour modes.

07 / IF SOMETHING STICKS

A little troubleshooting.

A game did not open
Allow site popups or switch to “Inside this page”. A game whose provider blocks embedding or is offline may be unavailable inside the arcade. The site does not redirect you to another game website.
AI will not connect
Allow the Puter sign-in popup, then try Connect again. If the SDK failed to load, press Reload AI service. A network filter can block the AI service.
AI stops or reports a limit
Use Retry after a connection error. For usage limits, check your Puter account. A request times out after two minutes; no automatic paid upgrade occurs in the arcade.
Cover shortcut does not work
External iframes receive their own keyboard input. Click the arcade toolbar and try again, or use Cover. Your browser may reserve some shortcuts.
Things disappear on another device
Notes, favourites, custom games, bookmarks and appearance settings are browser-local. Private browsing and clearing site data can remove them. Export anything important.
An update is missing
Reload the page. GitHub Pages can take a short time to publish a new commit. If needed, use your browser’s reload-without-cache option.
08 / SITE ADMINISTRATION

Administration & privacy.

Admin panel is visible only to a configured administrator account. It shows account names, account creation dates, last-seen times, recent page visits and game totals. Guests appear anonymously; the panel does not identify them. Visit records expire after 30 days. Passwords are hashed and are never shown, collected by analytics or returned by the admin API.

The site owner must designate an existing arcade username before administrator access is enabled. Register through Account, then supply only that username to the owner. The panel stays locked until configured.

Friends, profiles & private messages

Open Friends & DMs. Add someone using their exact arcade username. They must accept your request before either person can send DMs. Incoming requests have Accept and Decline controls; outgoing requests can be cancelled. Removing a friend stops further DMs until a new request is accepted.

Use Edit profile to choose your display name, a preset avatar, an accent colour, a banner and a bio of up to 240 characters. Click a username in chat, the friend list or a leaderboard to view their profile. Usernames remain fixed.

DMs are stored by the shared service and are visible only to the two accepted friends through the app. They are not end-to-end encrypted. The latest 100 messages load per conversation. Reported messages are shared with the administrator for review. Avoid posting sensitive personal information.

Choose Block player from a profile to stop DMs, friend requests and shared party access, and hide their public messages from your view. Blocking removes the friendship. Unblocking from the blocked list does not restore it automatically.

Online party rooms

Open Party rooms, choose a game and create a room. Share the 10-character code, or use Invite a friend to send it in a DM. Your friend enters that code and joins. The host is Player 1 and moves first. Rooms support exactly two players and expire after 24 hours.

Integrated online rooms support Chess, Tic Tac Toe, Connect Four and Dots & Boxes. Compatible catalogue cards have a Play online with a friend button, and Chess Club has a Play a friend online button. Other games retain their own local or provider multiplayer controls; external game embeds cannot be synchronized by this site.

Moves and turns are validated by the server. Rooms refresh about every 2.5 seconds while visible; use Refresh if needed. Chess supports legal moves, promotion choices and draws. Dots & Boxes awards an extra turn after capturing a box. Leaving an unfinished room ends the game for both players. Recent rooms can be resumed from the party page. No voice chat is included.

Daily challenge & leaderboards

Open Daily challenge for the shared Lights Out puzzle. Clicking a tile toggles it and its four direct neighbours. Switch off every light, then submit your moves. The server replays them before accepting a result.

The puzzle changes at midnight in Australia/Sydney. Undo removes your last move; Try again starts a fresh attempt. Your best result per day earns max(1, 1000 − 5 × moves) points. Today’s leaderboard ranks fewer moves first, then the earliest initial submission. All-time standings sum each player’s best daily points. Retrying improves your result without adding duplicate daily points. These are puzzle rankings, not rankings for external games.

Reports & moderation

Use Report on a chat message, received DM or player profile, then describe what needs review. The report stores a copy of the reported content so it can still be reviewed if the original public message is removed. Only the administrator can read the report queue; reporting does not notify the reported player.

Administrators open Moderation from the sidebar or Admin panel. They can review reports, mark them resolved or dismissed, delete reported public/server messages, set public lobby slow mode (0–300 seconds), and mute an account for 10 minutes, one hour or 24 hours.

Community suspension blocks posting, DMs, friend requests, room actions and challenge submissions. It does not block sign-in or reading. Choose No mute and clear Suspend community features to restore access. The designated administrator cannot be restricted through this panel. The panel shows only DMs submitted with a report, not a general DM inbox.

Passwords & recovery codes

New accounts receive a private recovery code after signup. Copy or download it and keep it outside this website. It is shown only in that view and is not saved in browser storage. Anyone with the code can reset that account’s password.

Existing players can open Account → Password & recovery code, confirm their current password, and generate a new code. This replaces any earlier backup or temporary code. Change password also requires the current password.

If you forget your password, open Account → Forgot password?, enter your username and recovery code, then choose and confirm a new password of at least 7 characters. A successful reset signs out all sessions and shows a replacement recovery code. Save that new code before closing it. Used codes cannot be reused.

If you have no saved code and cannot sign in, the site administrator must independently verify your identity. The administrator opens Account → Issue player reset code (also available in Admin panel), supplies the player’s username, confirms their own admin password, and acknowledges the identity check. The generated code expires after 15 minutes, works once, and can be entered in Forgot password. An administrator-issued code cannot target the designated admin account. Administrators use their own password or saved backup code.

No password or recovery code is emailed. Codes are never placed in links. Only code hashes are stored; the old password remains unreadable. The admin issuance process does not show or choose the player’s new password. Keep recovery codes out of public chat.
