01 / PERMISSION COMES FIRST

Your school’s rules still apply.

Use Pixel Arcade only when your teacher, school, parent or device owner allows it. Follow their acceptable-use policies and instructions. Pixel Arcade is an independent project and is not endorsed by your school.

Do not blame the owner of this GitHub repository if you get caught playing against your teacher’s instructions, except where the law requires otherwise.

Study view, Cover, tab appearance settings and Quick exit change what is visible on your screen. They do not hide network traffic, erase browsing history, bypass filters or guarantee that your activity cannot be seen by teachers or device administrators. Do not use these controls to ignore school rules. Study view closes games in this tab; it does not control separately opened tabs.

02 / BE GOOD TO EACH OTHER

Keep the community welcoming.

Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, scam or share someone else’s private information. Do not post unlawful or exploitative material, attempt to access another account, interfere with the service or abuse rewards. Use Report on a message or profile when something needs review.

Administrators and authorized moderators can review reports and use the moderation controls described in the manual. Responses may include removing messages, slowing chat or restricting community features. Custom cosmetic roles and marketplace titles do not grant administrator access.

03 / YOUR ACCOUNT & YOUR DATA

Protect your account.

Use a password you do not share with other people or reuse elsewhere. Keep recovery codes private. If you are under the age at which you can consent to a service, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. Follow the age rules of any external service you choose to use.

Account passwords are stored as one-way hashes and are not shown to administrators. Profiles and public chat are visible to other signed-in players. Private messages are available to the two accepted friends through the app, but are not end-to-end encrypted; reported messages and their snapshots can be reviewed by authorized moderators.

The service records account creation, visits and signed-in game activity for site administration. Guests appear anonymously in the admin panel. Visit records expire after 30 days. Account, chat, moderation and wallet records are stored by the shared service. Hosting and external providers may process technical request information under their own policies.

Notes, favourites and appearance preferences are saved in your browser. Stay signed in saves a session token on this browser for up to 30 days, not your password. Logging out clears that saved session and revokes its token. Browser-local data can disappear if you clear storage or use private browsing. See the user manual for recovery and data details.

04 / REWARDS, WITHOUT MONEY

Coins are for this arcade.

Coins and cosmetics have no cash value, cannot be redeemed for money and are not an investment. Pixel Arcade does not sell coins or offer random paid rewards. Reward amounts, daily limits and equipment rules are shown in Marketplace and the manual.

A referral awards the inviter 1,500 coins once when a new account uses their link or username at signup. Existing accounts cannot add a referrer later. Do not create duplicate accounts or automate signups, chatting or activity to farm coins. Grants and purchases are recorded in the server wallet ledger. Administrators may review suspected abuse; material reward-rule changes will be described in the updated site instructions.

05 / OTHER CREATORS & SERVICES

Respect the original creators.

Some games, browser connections, AI and media use external providers. Their availability, accounts, content, licences and rules are controlled by those providers. Loading them can share technical information with the provider. Only upload or link content you have the right to use, and respect intellectual-property rights.

Pixel Arcade cannot guarantee external games, embeds, AI or browser connections will load, work on your device or be available on a school network. Do not use the site to bypass access controls. Some games require large downloads or powerful hardware; read their device notices before launching.
